Course objectives

The aim of this practical course is for the student to design and conduct their own linguistic experiment, based on their knowledge of basic quantitative methods, for which they will collect real linguistic data.

The experiments are focused on the word in the text, its properties, different ways of grasping it, and its relations with other linguistic units.

Student Requirements

  • Study of literature in the field of text analysis
  • Collection of their own linguistic material applicable to the selected experiment
  • Conducting the selected experiment with their own linguistic material
  • Report on the conducted experiment and interpretation of the obtained outputs
